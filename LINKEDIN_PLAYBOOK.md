## **ENGAGEMENT TACTICS**

**Posting frequency:**
- 3-4 posts per week (consistency > volume)
- Optimal times: 8am (France) or 5pm (global B2B)
- Mix: 60% educational, 30% company/credential, 10% engagement/polls

**Comment engagement:**
- Reply to ALL comments within 24 hours
- Expand on replies (2-3 sentences minimum)
- Like + reply on industry posts (builds visibility)
- Tag relevant experts (builds network)

**LinkedIn algorithm boost:**
- Post → save (target 10+ saves = algorithm boost)
- Post → comment (target 5+ comments in first hour)
- Post → share (target 2-3 shares)
- Post → click (track link clicks via UTM: ?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=post)
