[SCREEN SHARE: Graphic showing emergency exits + checklist items]

[YOU voiceover, clear and direct]

"Exigence numéro un: Sorties de secours.

Article R.4227-28 dit:

Minimum deux sorties.

Largeur minimale — dépend du nombre occupants.

Clearly marked. Signage en français.

Always unobstructed. Jamais bloquer.

Accessible for everyone. Personnes handicapées incluses.

Moi je vérifie: Les portes peuvent-elles s'ouvrir sans clé? Elles sont dégagées? Signalétique visible et en français?

### [1:30-2:45] REQUIREMENT 2: FIRE SUPPRESSION

[SCREEN SHARE: Fire suppression equipment graphic (sprinklers, extinguishers, etc.)]

"Exigence deux: Suppression incendie.

Options:

Sprinkler system. Pour bâtiments plus grands — généralement plus de cinq cents mètres carrés.

Extinguishers. Pour bâtiments plus petits.

Both. Pour ERPs très grands.

Moi je vérifie:

Equipment presence. Sont-ils là?

Locations. Accessible?

Maintenance logs. Inspection annuelle minimum?

Staff knows how to use. Formation?

Les types d'extincteurs:

ABC — multi-usage, plus commun.

CO2 — pour incendies électriques.

Water — standard.

Important: Le personnel doit être SSIAP 1 formé. C'est obligatoire.

Si equipment non-maintenu? Amende + responsabilité."

[B-ROLL: Close-ups of sprinklers, extinguishers, maintenance records, training scenarios]

### [2:45-3:45] REQUIREMENT 3: ALARM SYSTEMS

[SCREEN SHARE: Alarm system graphic (sensors, manual stations, speakers, battery backup)]

"Exigence trois: Systèmes d'alarme.

Checklist:

Audible in ALL zones. Chaque coin du bâtiment.

Tested monthly. Vérification mensuelle.

Manual pull stations visible et accessible. Pas caché.

Automatic detection — si bâtiment ancien, parfois exception.

Backup power supply. Battery backup. Trèès important.

Moi je teste: Je vais coin le plus loin du bâtiment. J'entends l'alarme?

Is backup power working? Je vérify batterie?

Si alarm system non-fonctionnel? C'est major non-compliance.

People won't know there's fire. Catastrophe."

[B-ROLL: Close-ups of alarm panels, pull stations, speakers, backup battery systems, testing procedures]

### [3:45-5:00] REQUIREMENT 4: STAFF TRAINING

[SCREEN SHARE: Training certification graphic (SSIAP 1, certificates, drills, first aid)]

"Exigence quatre: Formation personnel.

Mandatory:

SSIAP 1. Minimum pour designated safety staff. C'est certification gouvernementale.

Annual evacuation drills. Tous les ans.

Emergency procedure training. Tout le staff doit connaître.

First aid certification. Recommandé mais pas obligatoire selon loi.

Moi je vérifie:

Certificates on file? Les certifications, elles existent?

Recent training? Pas vieux de cinq ans?

Staff can articulate evacuation plan? Je pose des questions. Ils savent quoi faire?

C'est critical. Pourquoi?

Fire = panic. Si personnel trained = calm, organized evacuation = lives saved.

Untrained personnel = chaos = tragedy."

[B-ROLL: Certificate examples, training in progress, evacuation drill footage, staff interviews, emergency procedures]

### [5:00-6:00] REQUIREMENT 5: DOCUMENTATION

[SCREEN SHARE: Documentation checklist (building plans, logs, records, files)]

Everything must be:

Building plans with exits marked. Visuel clair.

Equipment maintenance logs. Sprinklers, alarms, extinguishers — tous documented.

Training certificates. SSIAP 1, annual drills, first aid.

Inspection records. Past audits, compliance checks.

Incident logs. Si any fire incidents — must be documented.

Evacuation drill records. Quand, combien de temps, problèmes rencontrés.

Most important: Everything retrievable in less than five minutes for inspector.

Why? Inspectors come unannounced. You must prove compliance immediately.

If documentation missing? Non-compliance. Amende."
