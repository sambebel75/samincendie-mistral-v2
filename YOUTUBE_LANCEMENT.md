### VIDEO 2: "Checklist Conformité ERP - Ce qu'il faut vérifier"

**Format:** Screen share (show checklist) + talking head
**Duration:** 6-7 min
**Goal:** Educational value + citability (AI crawlers love lists)

```
SCRIPT (178 mots - citability optimized)

[INTRO - 0:00-0:15]
Vous gérez ERP? Bâtiment public?
Voici checklist conformité incendie française.
Je vais tout décortiquer.

[REQUIREMENT 1: EMERGENCY EXITS - 0:15-1:30]
Exigence 1: Sorties de secours.

Article R.4227-28 dit:
✓ Minimum 2 sorties
✓ Largeur minimum (dépend occupancy)
✓ Clearly marked, signage en français
✓ Unobstructed always
✓ Accessible to everyone (disabled included)

Je vérifie: portes peuvent-elles s'ouvrir sans clé? Sont-elles dégagées? Signalétique visible?

Cost if non-compliant: €75k fine + fermeture possible.

[REQUIREMENT 2: FIRE SUPPRESSION - 1:30-3:00]
Exigence 2: Suppression incendie.

Options:
- Sprinkler system (buildings >500 m² typically)
- Extinguishers (smaller buildings)
- Both (large ERPs)

Je vérifie:
✓ Equipment presence + location
✓ Maintenance logs (annual inspection minimum)
✓ Staff knows how to use
✓ Accessible (not blocked)

Types d'extincteurs:
- ABC (all-purpose, most common)
- CO2 (electrical fires)
- Water (standard)

Staff must be SSIAP 1 trained.

[REQUIREMENT 3: ALARM SYSTEMS - 3:00-4:15]
Exigence 3: Systèmes d'alarme.

✓ Audible in ALL zones
✓ Tested monthly
✓ Manual pull stations visible + accessible
✓ Automatic detection if building age permits
✓ Backup power supply (battery backup)

Je teste: Can I hear alarm from farthest corner? Is backup power functional?

[REQUIREMENT 4: STAFF TRAINING - 4:15-5:45]
Exigence 4: Formation personnel.

Mandatory:
- SSIAP 1 (minimum) for designated safety staff
- Annual evacuation drills
- Emergency procedure training
- First aid certification (recommended)

Je vérifie: Certificates on file? Recent training? Staff can articulate evacuation plan?

[REQUIREMENT 5: DOCUMENTATION - 5:45-6:30]
Exigence 5: Dossiers complets.

Tenant:
- Building plans with exits marked
- Equipment maintenance logs
- Training certificates
- Inspection records
- Incident logs (if any)
- Evacuation drill records

Everything must be retrievable in <5 minutes for inspector.

[CALL-TO-ACTION - 6:30-7:00]
Checklist complet disponible samincendie.fr.
Besoin audit professionnel? Contactez-moi.
```
