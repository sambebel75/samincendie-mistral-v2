Fire Safety Audit & Diagnosis

A complete on-site audit to identify every regulatory gap and give you a clear, prioritized, actionable plan.

Request a free audit
Methodology

Our three-phase approach

01

Document review

Review of the safety register, periodic inspection reports, evacuation plans, fire safety instructions and safety commission minutes.

02

On-site inspection

Full walkthrough of your establishment: equipment checks (extinguishers, fire safety system, smoke extraction, emergency lighting), egress routes, signage and electrical installations.

03

Report & action plan

Delivery of a detailed report rating each risk (critical / major / minor), a prioritized action plan and budget estimates for each corrective measure.

What we check

The 10 key points of our audit

Fire-fighting equipment

  • Portable extinguishers (compliance, signage, maintenance)
  • Fire hose reels
  • Dry and wet risers
  • Automatic suppression systems (sprinklers)

Fire Safety System (SSI)

  • Automatic fire detectors
  • Manual call points
  • Fire safety control panel (CMSI)
  • Alarm system (type 1 to 4)

Smoke extraction & evacuation

  • Smoke vents and hatches
  • Egress routes and emergency exits
  • Emergency lighting (BAES/BAEH)
  • Evacuation signage

Documentation & organization

  • Fire safety register
  • Posted evacuation plans
  • Written fire safety instructions
  • Up-to-date staff training records
