📋 Legal obligation (France)

Article R.4227-28 of the French Labour Code requires every employer to take the necessary steps so that any fire outbreak can be quickly and effectively fought. This makes training staff designated as first-response fire wardens mandatory.

Fire awareness training — All staff

Awareness training for all staff, no prerequisites. Understand fire risks, the right behaviors, and how to use first-aid firefighting equipment.

  • Duration: 2 hours to half a day
  • Theory: fire triangle, fire classes
  • Practice: extinguisher handling
  • Evacuation instructions and assembly points
