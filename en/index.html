Is your ERP establishment
actually fire compliant?

A public establishment (ERP) in France — hotel, restaurant, school, shop, clinic, gym — must meet fire safety standards under the French Labour Code (Article R.4227-28): adequate emergency exits, working detection and suppression systems, clear signage, trained staff, an up-to-date safety register. Non-compliance exposes the operator to administrative closure, fines of up to €75,000, and criminal liability in the event of an incident. An SSIAP 3 expert audit identifies gaps and delivers an action plan to reach compliance. Fast turnaround: free quote within 24h.
